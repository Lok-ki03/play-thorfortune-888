Start in four steps

  1. Set a stake. Enter the amount you want to risk in the round; every multiplier is applied to this figure.

  3. Reveal tiles. Tap any tile — a gem increases your multiplier, while a mine ends the round straight away.
  4. Cash out in time. Lock in the current return whenever you like; patience matters, but so does discipline.

3. Is there a best number of mines?
